# Source and contribution notes

The public files contain original research summaries and sanitized observations. Downloaded BMW firmware, coding datasets, diagnostic software, third-party manuals, forum copies, and private conversations are excluded.

Source references identify the material studied. They do not transfer rights to third-party material. No reuse licence has been selected for the original repository text.

A useful contribution states which finding it supports or contradicts, the evidence level, and the limits. Do not submit personal identifiers, credentials, raw conversations, or proprietary datasets.
